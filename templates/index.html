SAM3 Image Segmentation

How to use:

  • Upload an image using the upload area below
  • Select point mode: Positive (green) for foreground, Negative (red) for background
  • Click on the image to add points
  • Click on existing points to remove them
  • Click "Generate Mask" to see the segmentation result
  • Click "Save Result" to download the mask and combined image (you can choose save location)

Click to select an image or drag and drop

Supported formats: JPG, PNG, JPEG
